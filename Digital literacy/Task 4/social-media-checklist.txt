✅ Do’s:

	•	Maintain a professional tone in your posts and comments

	•	Think before posting any content online

	•	Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication

	•	Verify information before sharing it

	•	Respect others’ opinions and communicate politely


❌ Don’ts:

	•	Do not share personal or sensitive information publicly

	•	Avoid posting offensive or inappropriate content

	•	Do not engage in online arguments or harassment

	•	Avoid spreading unverified news or rumors

	•	Do not use social media during important academic or professional work
