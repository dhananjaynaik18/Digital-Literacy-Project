Stay Safe Online: Your UPI & Cyber Safety Checklist
• The golden rule: You never have to enter your PIN to receive money. If someone tells you to enter your PIN so they can pay you, it's a scam.
• Keep your info private: Never share your UPI PIN, OTPs, or bank details with anyone, no matter who they claim to be on the phone.
• Ignore random QR codes: If a stranger sends you a QR code to scan, just don't do it.
• Watch out for sketchy links: Steer clear of random links in your texts or emails promising unexpected payments, cashbacks, or special offers.
• Double-check the receiver: Always take a quick second to verify exactly who you are sending money to before confirming the payment.
• Stick to the real apps: Only use official, trusted payment apps downloaded from official app stores.
Quick UPI-Specific Tips
• Read the prompt carefully: Always double-check if the screen says "Pay" or "Receive" before you hit approve. Scammers rely on you being in a rush!
• Block unknown requests: Just decline or ignore payment requests from contacts you don't recognize.
What to do if you get scammed
If the worst happens and you do get tricked, act fast. Don't panic, just do these two things immediately:
• Report it right away on the National Cyber Crime Portal at cybercrime.gov.in.
• Call the Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930 as quickly as you can.
